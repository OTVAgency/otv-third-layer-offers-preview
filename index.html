BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A few good questions.

Which offer is right for my business?+

Choose Launch Ready if you have existing brand assets and need a professional website. Choose Lead Flow if your priority is capturing and following up on inquiries with your existing brand. Choose Signature Launch if you want a fuller brand refresh, social templates, and website developed together. Signature Launch does not include Lead Flow’s automatic inquiry steps. We’ll confirm the fit in our first conversation.

Does this include a new logo?+

Launch Ready uses your existing logo or brand assets. Signature Launch includes a logo or visual identity refresh. An entirely new or expanded identity is separately scoped. Lead Flow does not include brand work.

What do I need to provide?+

Plan to provide approved website text, images, existing brand files, relevant account access, and one person who can make decisions and give feedback. The packages cover one business and one language. If you need help creating content, we can discuss it as an additional service.

How much will my project cost?+

Request a project quote. We’ll review your needs and confirm the deliverables, price, schedule, revision rounds, and payment terms in writing before work begins. Added pages, domain charges, software, hosting, print production, and other services are separate unless your quote includes them.

Will Lead Flow work with my current tools?+

Lead Flow connects one customer-management, form, or intake tool you already own, with up to two simple automatic steps. We’ll confirm compatibility before agreeing to the project. Additional apps, automatic steps, booking, dashboards, and extra reporting are separately scoped. Software subscriptions are paid directly by you.

How long will it take?+

We’ll agree on a schedule after reviewing your scope, materials, and both teams’ availability. The timing of your feedback and approvals also affects the schedule.

Can you help after launch?+

Hosting, website care, and support for inquiry follow-up can be discussed separately. Any ongoing plan will have its own scope, cost, and support terms.
